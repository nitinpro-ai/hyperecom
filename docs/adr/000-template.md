# ADR — Architecture Decision Record

- **ADR number:** NNN
- **Title:** short title
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNN
- **Deciders:** names/roles

## Context

What is the problem? What forces are in play (constraints, requirements,
deadline pressure, team skills)? What options were considered?

## Decision

What we will do — stated plainly. Reference the spec section(s) it changes
(`docs/spec/…`), and update the decision log in `docs/spec/00-README.md`
(D-xxx) if it affects a locked decision.

## Consequences

- Positive effects
- Negative effects / risks accepted
- What becomes harder or impossible later
- Mitigations and review triggers (e.g. re-evaluate at 100k listings)

## References

Spec sections, vendor docs, discussions.
